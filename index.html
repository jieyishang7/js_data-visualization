About this reproduction & data

The 62 countries shown have at least 20 million people in the original public dataset. Areas represent their share of the displayed population, not the entire world. Values are rounded mid-2023 projections.

This independently written weighted Voronoi layout matches the circular composition, regional grouping, colors, and label hierarchy. Individual polygon boundaries and some label positions differ from the original. Source classifications are preserved, including Egypt in Middle East and Russia in Europe.
