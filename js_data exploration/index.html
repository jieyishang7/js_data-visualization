How to read the chart

Longer bars indicate a higher average life evaluation. Thin whiskers show the reported 95% confidence interval for that average.

Small rank differences can accompany overlapping intervals. Overlap alone is not a formal test of whether two countries differ.

The score summarizes residents’ own 0–10 ratings of their lives. It is not calculated by adding six raw economic and social indicators.

What stands out?

The highest 20 scores span 0.943 points; the lowest 20 span 2.860 points. Switch between the groups to compare their spread on the same 0–10 axis.

The full workbook contains 2,116 rows across 14 year labels. This first chart uses only its latest 147 rows (Year = 2025), which correspond to the 2026 report’s 2023–2025 ranking window. All 147 have complete scores and confidence intervals.

Source: World Happiness Report, Figure 2.1 data. University of Oxford Wellbeing Research Centre; underlying life evaluations from the Gallup World Poll. Accessed October 13, 2026.
