Happiness & explanatory factors

Where do countries sit when a modeled contribution is compared with their observed happiness score?

The x-axis shows the report’s modeled contribution in score points, not raw GDP, years of life, or survey percentages. These contributions are part of a fitted decomposition. Patterns here do not establish causation or independently identify the strongest raw factor.

One point per country or territory, 2023–2025. Hover, focus or tap a point for its exact contribution, score and 95% confidence interval. Missing values are excluded only for the selected contribution. No fitted line is shown.

Reading the evidence

Life evaluation is measured on a 0–10 scale. Scores are country averages, not descriptions of every resident. Survey coverage varies, uncertainty matters, and overlapping windows are not independent annual observations. The six modeled contributions are explanatory estimates, not proven causal effects.
